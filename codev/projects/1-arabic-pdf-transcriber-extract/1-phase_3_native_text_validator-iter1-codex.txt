KEY_ISSUES:
- `validate_page()` accepts pages whose native extraction is effectively empty as long as `has_text_layer=True` (`src/arabic_pdf_transcribe/validate/native_validator.py:217-259`). For example, whitespace-only text yields `accept=True`. That violates the phase objective of rejecting native output that is “missing or insufficient” and should be covered by a test.
